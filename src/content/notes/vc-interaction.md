VC that are good at investment are really sharp when it comes to founder and business potential. No one care about who we are and what we have now, it is about our potential in the long term. Whether we can achieve something of significant value in the future.

If a VC cannot see this potential, or if you are unsuccessful in conveying this message. It is often quite normal for VC to show high level of uninterestedness to the point of being blatantly rude and disrespectful (such as ignoring you while still in the meeting and talking among themselves instead.)
