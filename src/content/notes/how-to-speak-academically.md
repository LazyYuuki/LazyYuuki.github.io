title: How to speak (academically)
published: 2026-08-02
---

- Start with a PROMISE => promise listener that they will gain something at the end of the talk
- Speak in cycle => important idea need to be repeated three times (not repeatedly, but circling back now and then throughout the talk)
- Put a fence around your idea => make sure your idea get its own recognition, and not just something akin to another more or less popular idea
- Verbal punctuation => give listener a way to get on the bus after they have lost track of what you are talking about => ordered list works
- Ask a question to re-engage => wait max 7 second for an answer, a question can't be too hard or too easy, keep it engaging
- Inspire other => always exhibited passion about what we are presenting => that is how to inspire other
- Ideas are like your children => don't let them goes into the world in rags
- You can get used to being famous => you will never get used to being ignore
- That is why you need to get a bit famous for what you are doing, it holds gravitas
- How to get your work remembererd?
    - Symbol => something easy to remember and resemble of your work
    - Slogan => some short line that quickly describe your work and catchy (could be trendy and transformative)
    - Surprise => it has to have an element of suprise to the conclusion
    - Salient => it has to be an idea that stick out from other
    - Story => it has to tell a story about its origin and contributions
