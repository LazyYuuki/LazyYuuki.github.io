## Delivery

- If you want to seem more authoritative, you need to pause more.
- Slow down your speech so it does not tire the listener out. It also gives you more time to think and articulate your ideas.
- Make declarative statements instead of rambling on and on. This brings the talk to the point and makes you seem authoritative.

## Vocal

- Warm up your voice before you use it. Talking is like playing a sport, and your vocal cords are muscles. You have to warm them up before you use them.
- Volume translates to vitality. To transfer energy, use your volume in the right manner.

## Cognitive

- Finish one thought at a time. It makes you more confident and clearer in communication.
- Use a conversational framework to help orient your brain. For example: Context, Core, and Connect.
- Use analogies to help explain things that are hard to understand.
