published: 2026-08-02
---

It is important when starting a company or doing something to increase your presence is being aware of how other people perceive you and not only just how you perceive yourself. While knowing yourself is important, it is equally important to portray what you want other to perceive of you also.

Sometimes what seems impressive and meaningful to us can send the wrong message to a group of people who holds power that can influence your life trajectory. And as long as you are not powerful enough to change that narrative your way, you are stuck keep playing the sucker game. And to get there, you need to first master the very game you want to avoid. And it starts with managing you self-image and other perception of you and who you are.
