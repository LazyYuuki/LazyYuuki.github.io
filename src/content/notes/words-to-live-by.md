---
title: Words to live by 
published: 2026-08-01
---

1. Don't bother your founder with fight, only notify them when the fight is escalating out of control, if it is resolved, just don't bother them

2. Never be the middle man to information keep it somewhere that everyone has access to so you are not the holder of knowledge

3. When you do transfer knowledge, just do it immediately so that you don't forget and keep information in your head again

4. Create good process so the team can always follow and refer to, you should never a bottleneck for information.

5. Don't bother the engineer team with unecessary ticket or sync, engineering time is precious, safe guard it for them.

6. Though 80% of the time, we should identify & solve the underlying issue, rather than create a new process on top of some underlying problem.

7. Train yourself to jump on critical problem instead of trying to suppress it for other reason. If there is critical problem, just jump on it and execute immediately.

8. Be very big level and strategic when we are talking business. Save the engineer mode for when you talk about technical stuff to technical people.

9. Don't just feel, have a concrete explanation on why thing is good and is bad, so that you can analyze things objectively

10. Having a timeline that is organised help with fundraising by showing them how we move.

11. Your main job is to prepare when you are not in war to go around and test and study your competitor and see how you can position yourself strategically.

12. It is important to remember that when you success, it is not because you are special. It is because other bigger people want to help you to succeed. Always remember what cards do you have and play them right.

13. Sounds obvious, but people only care about things that get done, not how it get done. So focus on reducing the step and optimizing for step ratio to thing get done. Less step => better experience.

14. TPF is about “doing things right” internally—making sure the product is feasible and effective given the current technology. PMF is about “doing the right things” for users—ensuring the product solves real problems and is embraced by the market.

15. You can only succeed, if the people around you want to help you succeed. So live in a way, that would make other want to help you.
