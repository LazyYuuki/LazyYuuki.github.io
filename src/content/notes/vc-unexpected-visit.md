---
title: Unexpected VC visit
published: 2026-08-02
---

VC will often show up unnoticed to learn about how the team function and identify the key player in a company. Ultimately, a VC invest more than in just an idea or a product at a company, they are investing in the team that is going to make it happened.

In order to increase confidence for investor, make the keyplayer in the company known and what they are capable of because ultimately they are betting on the team to succeed and not just only the founder or the products.

Often time, a successful start-up will also have its early employee spawn off to create their own new venture. So VC will also take note of that and so it is important for employee to make themselves known also.
