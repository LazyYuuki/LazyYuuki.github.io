---
title: Perception as motivation
published: 2026-08-02
---

Perception of oneself is a crucial driver for motivation. How you perceive yourself will form the motivation for you to pursue a certain thing or not.

To gain more motivation, it is important to first think of a way to fix / improve your perception of yourself. If you think of yourself as someone who cannot do a certain thing, then by your own will you will never have the motivation to do it.
