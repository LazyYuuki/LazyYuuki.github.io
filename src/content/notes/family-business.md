---
title: Family office is not just for family
published: 2026-08-02
---

It is important to understand the local business context of where you live and grow your career. Nothing is as simple as it seems. 

Always see when a big name appears, how are they related in a bigger setting to each other. It is highly likely that they all come from the same family and hence exert the same sort of influence. When you can recognize these faces, these connections and people, you will naturally be able to play your card much better.
